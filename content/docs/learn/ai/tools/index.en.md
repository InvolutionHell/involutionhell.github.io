Student emails unlock a surprising number of free AI products.

So I decided to start a series sharing these AI products and my experience using them — to help everyone claw back the cost of tuition.

At the moment I can only think of 3 products worth covering; anything I can't recall probably isn't useful enough to mention.

Once I've written about these three, I'll likely expand beyond student freebies and share other free/paid tools that have genuinely helped me: Gemini CLI, Codex, and more.
